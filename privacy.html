Privacy Policy for Servo

Last updated: September 2026

Servo ("we," "our," or "the app") is a local services marketplace that helps customers find and contact verified service providers (such as plumbers, electricians, carpenters, and other home service professionals). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to the Servo Customer App, the Servo Worker App, and the Servo Admin Panel (collectively, "the Services").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and a securely stored password (managed via Firebase Authentication). We do not have access to your plain-text password.
  • Worker profile information: If you sign up as a service provider, we collect your name, service category, phone number, a short description of your work, and optionally a profile photo and photos of your previous work.
  • Ratings and reviews: If you rate a service provider, we collect the star rating and any comment you choose to submit.
  • Usage information: Basic information about how the app is used (such as which categories are viewed) may be collected to improve the Services.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, payment card details, or precise location tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display service provider profiles to customers browsing the app
  • To allow customers to contact providers directly via phone call or WhatsApp
  • To display ratings and reviews to help customers make informed decisions
  • To verify and approve service provider profiles before they are shown publicly
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the Services

3. How Your Information Is Shared

Some information is, by design, visible to other users of the app:

  • If you are a service provider, your name, category, phone number, description, rating, and any photos you upload are visible to customers browsing the app once your profile is approved.
  • If you are a customer submitting a rating, your rating and comment may be visible to the service provider and/or app administrators.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Your information may be shared with our service providers (such as Firebase/Google Cloud, which hosts our database, authentication, and file storage) solely to operate the app.

4. Data Storage and Security

Servo uses Google Firebase (Firestore Database, Firebase Authentication, and Firebase Storage) to store and secure user data. Firebase applies industry-standard security practices, including encrypted data transmission. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • You may update or edit your profile information (for service providers) at any time through the Worker App.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details below.
  • You may choose not to provide certain optional information, such as a profile photo or work photos.

6. Children's Privacy

Servo is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform

When you contact a service provider via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, as it takes place outside of the Servo app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
